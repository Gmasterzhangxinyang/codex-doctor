# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Codex Doctor to be a practical and welcoming project for developers debugging AI agent workflows.

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and careful with private data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sharing another person's private prompts, code, keys, logs, or reports without permission.
- Pressuring maintainers or users to disclose secrets or hidden model reasoning.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.
